## What Was Added

### New Taxonomy Codes (15)

Fifteen codes were added to close gaps identified in `reports/taxonomy-gap-analysis.md`, covering concepts from PHIL 4040 (Decision Theory & Strategic Interactions) lecture notes W9-W16:

- Pareto efficiency and social choice theory concepts
- Mixed strategies
- Signaling games
- Behavioral game theory (Ultimatum/Trust/Dictator games, level-k thinking)
- Folk Theorem

### Source Mappings

Source material passages from PHIL 4040 lecture notes were mapped to the new taxonomy codes in `source_loader.py`, enabling the adaptation and synthetic generation pipelines to produce content for the new codes.

### Adapted Problems (adapted_phil4040.jsonl)

46 schema-valid records were generated by adapting Type 1 and Type 2 problems from the PHIL 4040 lecture materials. All 46 records pass Pydantic validation via `schema.read_corpus()`.

### Cross-Reference Updates

225 existing corpus records received `C1.3` added to their `related_patterns` field, improving the cross-referencing between microeconomics and the newly added concepts.
